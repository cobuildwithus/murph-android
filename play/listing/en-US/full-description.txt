Murph is a private health companion. Sign in or create your Murph account with a phone or email one-time code, then choose Murph's contact-card look, personality, voice, and tone.

Connect Health Connect so Murph can use the categories you choose across activity and fitness, exercise, sleep, body measurements and vital signs, nutrition and hydration, and menstrual and reproductive health. Category availability depends on the device, membership, and apps writing to Health Connect. The app shows backend-confirmed connection status, helps you recover when permissions change, and lets you start a sync while the app is open.

You can also choose to share privacy-minimized Friendly Names from your Android contacts. Murph reads contacts only after you tap Share, Update, or Retry. This optional feature helps group conversations use familiar names; it does not send invitations or messages to your contacts.

You stay in control:

• Health Connect access is explicit and read-only.
• Contact sharing is optional and can be stopped from Settings.
• Privacy, consumer health data, account deletion, and support links are available in the app.
• Signing out tears down the local connected-health session before ending authentication.

Murph is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
